About TrustBridge

TrustBridge is an educational prototype that demonstrates how basic business information, such as monthly income, expenses, and business experience, can generate explainable demo indicators for micro-entrepreneurs.

It does not measure actual repayment behaviour or determine real creditworthiness.

How is this Trust Score calculated?

TrustBridge uses three demo indicators to calculate an overall score.

  • Ability to Repay (40%): Based on the business income and savings pattern.
  • Willingness to Repay (35%): Uses business experience as an illustrative indicator.
  • Stability of Activity (25%): Uses business experience as an illustrative indicator.

These weights and rules are for demonstration only. They are not validated lending criteria.

How TrustBridge Works

TrustBridge uses three demo indicators to create an easy-to-understand financial overview.

1. Ability to Repay

Looks at the relationship between monthly business income and expenses to calculate a demo savings indicator.

2. Willingness to Repay

Uses business experience as an illustrative indicator. Actual repayment behaviour is not measured in this demo.

3. Stability of Activity

Uses the duration of business experience as a simple demo indicator of business activity.

Note: These are illustrative demo rules, not validated financial or lending criteria.

Calculate Trust Score

Enter three demo indicator scores to calculate an overall score.

Demo weight: 40% — based on the income and savings indicator. Demo weight: 35% — uses business experience as an illustrative indicator. Demo weight: 25% — also uses business experience in this prototype.

Enter a value between 0 and 100 for each indicator. The calculator combines them using the displayed demo weights.

Demo Scoring Formula

Overall Score = (Ability × 0.40) + (Willingness × 0.35) + (Stability × 0.25)

This is an educational demonstration, not a real credit score or loan approval decision.
